# Roadmap

## Current status

The Public Beta combines Chrome scanning, JSON handoff, and Figma import in one versioned workflow. Verified capabilities are listed in each Release note.

## Next focus: component coverage and reconstruction accuracy

- Expand coverage of common controls and evidence-backed composite components.
- Improve relationships among states, icons, controls, menus, lists, and popovers.
- Improve stability on dynamic and deeply nested interfaces.
- Keep repeated imports predictable and reviewable.
- Continue anonymous automated testing and authorized real-site acceptance checks.

## Development principles

- Preserve only observed evidence and do not invent unobserved states.
- Do not substitute website-specific production rules for general behavior.
- Require automated verification and end-to-end Chrome + Figma acceptance before freezing a Release.
- Do not lower privacy or security gates to meet a schedule.

## Release timing

The next beta update will be released after the planned component-coverage improvements pass safety, quality, and acceptance checks. No fixed release date is promised.

## Boundaries

The roadmap does not promise support for every website, every component, or complete recovery of an internal design system. Priorities may change based on evidence and community feedback.
